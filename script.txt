Have you ever wondered how ChatGPT generates answers, AI creates images, or tools generate videos from just a text prompt?

The technology behind all this is called Generative AI, or GenAI.

Generative AI is a type of Artificial Intelligence that can create new content, such as text, images, videos, audio, and even code.

But how does it actually work?

AI models learn patterns from large amounts of training data. When you provide a prompt, they use those learned patterns to generate new content.

For example, ChatGPT generates text one token at a time, while image generation models can create pictures from text descriptions.

ChatGPT, AI image generators, video generators, and coding assistants are all examples of Generative AI.

And the best part? You can build your own GenAI applications using JavaScript, Python, and open-source models like Ollama.
